Our Heritage

The Story of SunPower Lanka: Journey to 1MW+

From our founding during national power challenges to orchestrating mega-scale industrial and residential clean power transformations.

☀️
Year 2021

The Genesis & Core Commitment

Founded during a critical period of electricity tariff hikes and fuel shortages in Sri Lanka. Our founding team set out with a definitive focus: bring authentic Bloomberg Tier-1 solar panels to Sri Lankan rooftops with transparent, mathematically proven ROI. Completed our first 50 residential systems in the Western province.

⚡
Year 2022

CEB / LECO Accreditation & Smart Hybrid Innovations

Secured official licensed renewable energy contractor status with the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) and LECO. Pioneered high-cycle LiFePO4 Lithium hybrid battery systems with seamless sub-10ms UPS switchover, keeping homes and medical facilities operating uninterrupted during prolonged national grid blackouts.

🏦
Year 2023

Green Bank Financing Alliances

Formed official vendor partnerships with Commercial Bank of Ceylon, Hatton National Bank (HNB), and Sampath Bank. Introduced low-interest green solar loans where a client’s monthly loan installment is actually lower than their previous CEB bill. Facilitated over Rs. 200 Million in consumer solar financing.

🌐
Year 2024

Sri Lanka's 1st Decentralized Dealer Leads Marketplace

Recognizing the need to support regional technicians, SunPower Lanka launched an islandwide partner portal. Local certified installers in Kandy, Galle, Kurunegala, Anuradhapura, and Jaffna were connected directly with verified homeowner inquiries, creating hundreds of local green energy jobs.

🏆
2025 - Present

Surpassing the 1 Megawatt (1,145+ kW) Landmark

Officially achieved over 1,145 kWp installed capacity across 560+ projects in 18 districts. Our footprint includes landmark industrial installations such as a 250kW tea factory in Nuwara Eliya, a 150kW export garment complex in Biyagama, and coastal resorts in Bentota, generating over Rs. 52,000,000 in annual client power savings!
