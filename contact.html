Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for CEB / LECO to connect the solar meter? ▼

Our physical installation is completed in just 1 to 2 days. The CEB or LECO grid approval and bi-directional smart meter installation typically takes 2 to 3 weeks depending on the regional CEB office. SunPower Lanka manages all paperwork from start to finish.

What happens during a national power cut? ▼

If you have a Smart Hybrid System with a Lithium battery, your power will not even flicker (switchover in less than 10 milliseconds). Your ACs, refrigerator, WiFi, and lights stay on. With a standard On-Grid System, the inverter shuts off safely to protect utility workers fixing lines (per CEB safety regulations).

Can solar panels survive heavy Sri Lankan monsoon rains, heat, and lightning? ▼

Yes! We exclusively install Tier-1 Bloomberg NEF panels rated for hail resistance, cyclone wind speeds up to 2400 Pa, and extreme tropical humidity. Every installation includes Type 2 DC and AC surge arrestors and copper earthing rods to protect against lightning spikes.

What is the difference between Net Metering, Net Accounting, and Net Plus? ▼

Net Metering: Daytime generation offsets nighttime consumption unit-for-unit. Excess units are carried forward as credit.
Net Accounting: If you export more than you consume, CEB pays you in cash for the surplus units at the fixed tariff rate.
Net Plus: 100% of the solar generated is sold directly to CEB at the approved feed-in tariff without offsetting your domestic meter.
